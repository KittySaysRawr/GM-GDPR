Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Last updated: 19/5/2026

GreenMeans is the data controller for any personal information processed through this website. We process data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

  • Entity: GreenMeans, a sole proprietor

We have determined that we do not currently require a Data Protection Officer (DPO). Any enquiries regarding your data or requests to exercise your rights should be made via the contact details in the Contact Us section below.

We collect minimal data to operate our website effectively:

Technical Data Limited We do not use bloated analytics. We use privacy-friendly analytics via Cloudflare Web Analytics, which respects user privacy.

We process data for the following purposes under the specified lawful bases:

Data Processing Purposes and Lawful Bases
Purpose Lawful Basis (UK GDPR) Details
Website Analytics Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) or Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) To analyse website traffic using privacy-first analytics (Cloudflare) that doesn't use cookies or track individual users.
Website Hosting & Security Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) To host, secure, and serve our website via GitHub with Cloudflare CDN and DDoS protection.
User Preferences (LocalSense) Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) To store your accessibility settings (e.g. dark mode, font choices) locally on your device using LocalSense. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.
Version Control Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) To manage website code using GitHub's platform.

Statutory/Contractual Requirements: You are under no statutory or contractual obligation to provide any personal data to us. Failure to provide technical data (such as IP addresses required for CDN delivery) may result in the website being inaccessible.

Automated Decision-Making & Profiling: We do not use any form of automated decision-making or profiling on this website.

  • Open standards (HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript ES6+)
  • Public APIs with transparent data practices
  • Privacy-respecting analytics that don't track individual users

All our service providers have appropriate safeguards in place for international data transfers and comply with applicable data protection laws.

Under UK data protection law, you have rights including:

  1. Right of access - You have the right to ask us for copies of your personal information.
  2. Right to rectification - You have the right to ask us to rectify personal information you think is inaccurate.
  3. Right to erasure - You have the right to ask us to erase your personal information in certain circumstances.
  4. Right to restriction of processing - You have the right to ask us to restrict the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  5. Right to object to processing - You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  6. Right to data portability - You have the right to ask that we transfer the personal information you gave us to another organisation, or to you, in certain circumstances.
  7. Right to withdraw consent - Where we rely on consent to process your data, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time.

You are not required to pay any charge for exercising your rights. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you.

To exercise any of these rights, please follow the instructions in our Contact Us section below.

Our website prioritises your privacy by avoiding unnecessary tracking:

  • Local Storage (LocalSense): We use localStorage to store your accessibility preferences (e.g., dark mode, font choices). This data remains exclusively on your device and is not used for tracking.
  • Privacy-Friendly Analytics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to provide aggregated, anonymised statistics to help us improve the site. This approach respects user privacy and avoids individual tracking.
